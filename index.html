Coming to The Kitchen Codex

Meet Shiso

Your Kitchen Codex companion.

We’re building one AI companion for your whole kitchen. You’ll be able to talk naturally with Shiso to create and adapt recipes, find favorites in your Vault, plan meals, build shopping lists, and explore nutrition.

Shiso will understand the question. The Kitchen Codex will do the math. For Advanced Nutrition, Shiso will work with Kitchen Codex systems that validate ingredients against USDA data and calculate nutrients from verified amounts — with your review before changes are applied.

Recipes are more than data.

They are family favorites and new discoveries. Traditions handed down and experiments still being perfected. The meals worth making again. The Kitchen Codex is being designed to give them a permanent, organized home — one vault, built around your kitchen.
