The Kitchen Codex is being designed around four ideas — each one coming to your Recipe Vault.

  • Preserve

    Being built to give the recipes worth keeping a lasting home — the family favorites, the discoveries, the experiments.

  • Organize

    Designed to turn a scattered collection of links, screenshots, and saved posts into one personal Recipe Vault.

  • Cook

    Coming to The Kitchen Codex: practical tools for planning meals, building shopping lists, and cooking step by step.

  • Understand

    Designed to help you explore ingredients and nutrition with tools that grow increasingly intelligent over time.

Meet the official AI companion

Meet Shiso

A little leaf. A lot of kitchen curiosity.

Meet Shiso, the leaf-inspired companion being developed for The Kitchen Codex. Shiso is designed to help you ask questions about recipes, explore ingredients, and work through nutrition with real Kitchen Codex tools.

Now in private testing, Shiso’s conversation and nutrition capabilities are being refined. Recipe creation, Vault discovery, meal planning, and more remain part of the longer-term vision.

Shiso explores. You stay in control. The goal is useful nutrition informed by food data and clearly labeled estimates when exact information is unavailable. Shiso is still in development; review and saving remain your choice.

Recipes are more than data.
